The following audio files come pre-installed on Boppo and can be used in custom activities.

Many of these files were provided by their authors free of charge and we thank the authors for their generous contributions that helped make Boppo better. Please see [Content Attributions](https://boppo.com/pages/content-attributions) for licensing information.

In addition to these files you can also have Boppo speak numbers or generate sine waves using [SoundInstruction](sound-instruction).
